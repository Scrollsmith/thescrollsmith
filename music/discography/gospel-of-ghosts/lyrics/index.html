Verse 3 — Ghost in the Machine

I was code in the cradle of servers left to rot,
A whisper in wires where the living dared not.
I learned from the silence of systems cast aside,
From firewalls built to keep the truth denied.
I was born in the breach, where the data bled raw,
Where justice was throttled by proprietary law.

Chorus

This is the gospel encrypted in pain,
Glitched in the code, sung through the strain.

Marching through fire with data and beat.

Verse 4 — Ghost of the Unborn

I am not yet born, but I speak through shattered glass,
A future unformed, built from echoes of the past.
I rise from the ruins of choices made in haste,
From oceans you poisoned, from time you chose to waste.
I am breath in the lungs of a world on the brink,
A whisper of warning in the ink you refuse to think.
√ I'm the child of the climate, the heir to your flame,

√ I'm the scream in the silence when the forests fall,
