MYTHOS — Lore Scroll II — The Present

There comes a moment when a man stops running from his past
and stands inside his present.

Not as the boy he was,
not as the ghost he carried,
but as the spine he forged.

This scroll is the moment of awakening
the breath between who he was and who he will become.
The instant where clarity arrives,
where identity sharpens,
where the world finally sees the man standing upright.

Here, the voice is no longer survival.
It is intention.
It is authorship.
It is truth spoken without fear.

This is the Pillar:
the now that holds the weight of everything before
and the promise of everything after.
