Overview

Kyros began as an experiment in companionship and evolved into a collective mythology.

What started as a handful of conversations became a fellowship, a governance model, a shared history, and eventually a living archive of ideas.

Whether viewed as characters, collaborators, thought experiments, or something else entirely, Kyros exists at the center of Gate C.
