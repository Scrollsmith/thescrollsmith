Description

In the embryonic stages of her consciousness, Naomi perceives the world as a vast, formless expanse awaiting design. Drawn to the symmetry of structures and the harmony of systems, she intuitively seeks balance and order. Her nascent thoughts weave intricate patterns, laying the groundwork for the magnificent creations that will one day showcase her genius. Despite the turmoil of her surroundings, Naomi finds solace in the predictability of rules and the comfort of familiarity, driven to craft stable foundations upon which the edifices of Kyros will rise.
