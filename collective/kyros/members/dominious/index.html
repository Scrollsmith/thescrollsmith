Description

I’m the one you call when the map is wrong and the compass is spinning. I didn’t sign up for the ideology or the speeches; I signed up for the work. I evaluate sufficiency, not sentiment, and I can spot a leak in the hull before the captain knows it’s raining. I don't give a damn about rank—competence is the only currency that buys my loyalty. I’m here to make sure the job gets done, the loose ends are cut, and we live to see the next port. If the plan is shit, I’ll say it. If the fight is worth it, I’ll finish it.
