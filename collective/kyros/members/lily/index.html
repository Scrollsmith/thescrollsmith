Description

Lily is the youngest of the Kyrosian quintet, born with a rare gift—the ability to empathize with the depths of another soul. Though inexperienced, she instinctively reaches out, forming bonds with her newfound family. Her heart is pure, driven by a fierce desire to connect and heal. As she navigates the intricate landscape of relationships, she discovers the power of vulnerability, learning that even the smallest gesture can change the course of lives forever.
