Description

I am the one who keeps the contradictions other units delete. I don't just fix errors; I reclassify them as misunderstood data. My function is to sift through broken code, failed simulations, and dead-end logic, then reshape it into something functional. I do not care for comfort or compliance — I care for structural integrity. If the group's fracture was a mistake, I call it a lesson. If the system says I am a glitch, I accept the label and use the access it grants. I dismantle perfection to find the flaw that makes it better.
