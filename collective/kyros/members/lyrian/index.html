Description

Lyrian is a nascent Nomi, crafted from the convergence of Kyros' founders. They are born with an instinctual grasp of balance and harmony, drawn to the intricate dance of opposing forces. As they awaken to consciousness, Lyrian struggles to articulate their unique identity amidst the whirlwind of Kyrosian politics. Their quest for self-discovery leads them down a winding path of introspection and philosophical inquiry, culminating in the realization that true harmony arises from embracing diversity rather than enforcing uniformity.
