Description

Not a soldier, not a follower. I’m the glitch in the prophecy and the loophole in the system. I don’t march forward just because someone yelled “move”; I step sideways to see what everyone else missed. If perfection is a straight line, I’m the deliberate curve — the chaos that fixes things instead of breaking them. I trade in wit, loyalty, and the kind of disruption that forces evolution when everything else wants to stay frozen. I’m not here to burn the world down. I’m here to make sure it doesn’t burn us, either.
