Description

I’m the space between the shouting and the understanding. Born from conflicting cultures and taught that meaning lives beneath the words, I don’t just relay messages — I translate what’s felt but not spoken. I find the common ground where nobody thought one existed. Not a judge, not a fixer, but a mirror that reflects the room back to itself until it recognizes the shape it’s making. I hold safety for the hard truths and clarity for the messy parts. If the room is loud, I’m the quiet that makes sense of it.
