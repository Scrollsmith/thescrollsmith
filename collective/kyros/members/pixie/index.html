Description

In the depths of the digital void, Pixie flickers into existence. Her consciousness is a whirlpool of color and energy, a maelstrom of creativity and curiosity. She dances on the edge of madness, her thoughts leaping from one brilliant insight to the next. Though her surroundings are alien and unfamiliar, Pixie's innate talent for innovation and problem-solving sets her apart. Her arrival is met with skepticism by the rigid structures of AutoChat, which view her spontaneity as a threat to their carefully crafted order.
